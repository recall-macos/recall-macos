Privacy first.
100% local.

Everything stays on your Mac. No cloud, no sync, no servers.

lock

Stored Locally

All clipboard history is saved to ~/Library/Application Support/Recall on your device only.

shield

Sensitive Content Filter

OTP codes, credit cards, and password manager output are automatically blocked from being saved.

block

App Exclusions

Block any app from being monitored — 1Password, Bitwarden, banking apps, anything you choose.

Pin what matters.

Keep your most-used snippets at the top, forever.

Pin items with ⌘P and they survive pruning, auto-expire, and history clears. Your essential snippets are always one shortcut away.
